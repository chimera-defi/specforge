# [Your Idea Name]

## Thesis
- What is this product and why does it matter?
- What's the core insight or opportunity?
- Why will this succeed where others haven't?

## Elevator Pitch
- 30-second pitch: what problem do you solve and for whom?
- What's the immediate value proposition?
- Why should someone care right now?

## Problem

### Current State
- What are people using now to solve this problem?
- What are the pain points with current solutions?
- Why are current solutions inadequate?

### The Problem
- What specifically is broken or missing?
- What's the gap you're filling?
- Why is this a problem worth solving?

## Why Now

### Timing
- Why is this the right moment?
- What changed in the market, technology, or user behavior?
- What's the catalyst?

## User & Market

### Target User
- Who specifically is this for?
- What's their job title or role?
- What's their context when using this?

### User Segment
- Which segment of the broader market?
- What's the specific niche?
- Why this segment first?

### Market Size
- How big is the opportunity?
- Is this a growing market?
- What's the TAM/SAM/SOM?

## Solution

### Approach
- How will you solve the problem?
- What's the core mechanism?
- What's the technical approach?

### Key Differentiator
- What makes you different from alternatives?
- What's your unfair advantage?
- Why can't competitors easily copy this?

## Technical Architecture

**Runtime Topology:** local-only | hosted-only | hybrid

**Release Stage:** alpha | beta | production

### Distribution Model
- How will users run this?
- Desktop app, web service, CLI, or combination?
- What's the deployment model?

### Agent Integration
- How do AI agents work with this?
- What's the agent interface?
- Can agents extend or automate this?

## Scope & Phases

### MVP Scope
- What's the minimum viable version?
- What's the smallest thing that delivers value?
- What can you ship in 4-6 weeks?

### Phase 1 Features
- What's in the first real release?
- What's the core feature set?
- What's the initial UX?

### Future Features
- What comes after MVP?
- What's the roadmap?
- What's the vision for v2, v3?

### Future Work
- What's the longer-term vision beyond the roadmap?
- What's the 2-5 year vision?
- What are the expansion opportunities?

### Non-Goals
- What are you explicitly NOT doing?
- What's out of scope for now?
- What will you never do?

## UX & Design

### Primary Surfaces
- What are the main interfaces?
- Web, mobile, CLI, desktop?
- What's the primary interaction model?

### Key Screens
- What are the critical screens?
- What's the happy path?
- What are the edge case screens?

### Failure States
- What can go wrong?
- How do you handle errors?
- What's the degradation strategy?

### Responsive
- Mobile, desktop, or both?
- What's the primary platform?
- What's the secondary platform?

## Verification

### Acceptance Tests
- How do you verify it works?
- What are the manual test cases?
- What are the automated tests?

### Success Metrics
- How do you measure success?
- What are the key KPIs?
- What's the definition of done?

## Risks & Constraints

### Technical Risks
- What could go wrong technically?
- What are the unknowns?
- What's your backup plan?

### Constraints
- What are your limitations?
- Time, budget, team, or technical constraints?
- What can't you change?
